    How it works

    The owner of a family-run Korean corn dog & boba shop has no time to post consistently. Got Tiger Social Studio plans, writes, checks and publishes posts, with the owner approving every one.

    AI agents (make decisions)

    1 · StrategistLooks up weather, holidays, menu, slow hours and past posts → decides item, platform and time
    ↓
    2 · WriterLoads brand voice + platform rules → drafts → self-checks → submits
    ⇅ up to 2 revision rounds
    3 · Brand ReviewerVerifies prices, promos, tone, length, repetition → approve / revise / reject

    Human checkpoint

    Owner approvalEdit, reschedule, approve or reject. Nothing publishes without this.

    Automation (fixed steps)

    Publishing automationTrigger: every 15 min or “publish now” → collect due → safety gate → publish → log & notify

    Where it can fail

    • Wrong facts: AI could invent a price or a deal. → Code checks every price/promo against the menu twice (reviewer + automation), and can overrule the AI.
    • Stale profile: if the menu or promos change and the profile isn't updated, checks pass on old data.
    • Tone misses: a joke that lands badly or a cultural misstep. → Owner reviews every post.
    • Photo mismatch: the post describes a photo the owner must still take.
    • Platform/API outages: a failed publish is held and the owner is notified, not silently dropped.
